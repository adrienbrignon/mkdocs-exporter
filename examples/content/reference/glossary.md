# Glossary

## Pages

Bleed
:   Artwork extending beyond the edge of the page, so that nothing white remains once it is trimmed.

Margin box
:   One of the sixteen boxes around the page area, holding headers, footers and page numbers ([CSS Paged Media](https://www.w3.org/TR/css-page-3/#margin-boxes)).

Named page
:   A kind of page, selected by the [`page`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/page) property and styled by `@page` rules of the same name.

Recto and verso
:   The right-hand (odd) and left-hand (even) pages of a book.

## Text

Running head
:   A header repeating the title of the current chapter or section.

Widows and orphans
:   Lines of a paragraph left alone at the top or at the bottom of a page, limited by the [`widows`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/widows) and [`orphans`](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/orphans) properties.
